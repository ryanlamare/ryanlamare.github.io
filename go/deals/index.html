APPLIED GAME THEORY · THE TWO DEALS

Two deals run through the whole programme

You will meet both of them in the games. This page is here so you can check back on either one at any point in the day.

TAPAS AIRWAYS

Madrid · the spare engine
  • Tapas Airways flies ten A330neos out of Madrid, and they need a spare engine
  • A spare Trent 7000 costs millions, and they want the best deal possible
  • Several funders are competing for this deal, and you are one of them
  • The airline knows things about its own position that you don’t
  • Get this deal, and you may get their business for the next decade
Your seatYou are one of the funders. The airline decides last.

GRANITO AIR

Parma · the five engines in storage
  • Granito Air flies seven A330ceos out of Parma, and they are retiring them. Five Trent 700 engines are sitting in storage
  • Those engines are worth millions, whole or in parts, and the airline wants the best deal possible
  • Several traders are competing for them, and you are one of them
  • The airline knows things about its own engines that you don’t
  • Get this deal, and there are more engines behind it – and maybe the whole fleet
Your seatYou are one of the traders. The airline decides last.
