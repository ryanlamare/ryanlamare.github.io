Every game carries its term from the moment it is recorded, so records accrue across years rather than weeks. Games only record while a term is set and every player picked their name off the roster — with no term the game still plays, it just does not count.

⚠️ The key is league-season, and the first segment is the league — ler565-2027-summer is LER 565's summer 2027 cohort, and every table on the records site groups by that first word. A league with no cohorts is just kitchen, with no season at all. Check the line above says what you meant before the first real game: the league is stamped onto every game the moment it records.

The board length is how many names the public leaderboard shows. Five of fourteen is a third of the room; five of thirty is a sixth, and thin enough that most people have nothing to chase. There is never a full public ranking.

Roster — no term set

Students pick their name off this list — no accounts, no passwords, no links to lose. Games with the instructor in them record as exhibitions and stay out of the league.

Each term keeps its own class list, so next year's cohort does not paste over this one — set the term above first, then paste. A player's id is not scoped to the term though: the same name means the same person across years, which is what lets a returning student keep one history and an all-time record book exist at all.

⚠️ A player's id comes from their name, so re-saving the same list is safe but renaming somebody starts them a fresh history. Fix spellings before week 1, not after.

Hall of Champions

The winner is worked out from the games — whoever won the last Cup game of the term — so there is nothing to declare here. What you set is what goes on their trophy: ask them which emblem they want and what line they want under it.
