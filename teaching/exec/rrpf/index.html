Optional pre-reading

No preparation is required, and the two days are self-contained. But if you would like a grounding beforehand, the core text below maps nicely onto our modules, is written for a general audience, and contains no mathematics beyond arithmetic.

Avinash K. Dixit and Barry J. Nalebuff, The Art of Strategy: A Game Theorist’s Guide to Success in Business and Life (W. W. Norton; paperback edition 2010, ISBN 978-0-393-33717-4).
