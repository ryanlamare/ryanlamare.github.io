THE RENEWAL
Alba and Batia · six annual renewal cycles

You are the commercial team at one of two suppliers, Alba and Batia. You sell an industrial component that your buyers regard as interchangeable: same specification, same lead times, and nothing much to choose between you beyond price. The same set of buyers renews annually, and every year both of you are invited to quote.

Neither of you has any other competitor to worry about, and neither of you is going anywhere. You will be quoting against each other again next year, and the year after that, for the next six years.

Your two choices

Option one

Hold your price

Quote at your standard rate. You keep your margin intact, and you keep your share of the buyers who renew with you, provided the other side does the same.

Option two

Undercut

Come in below them. If they hold, you take volume off them and finish the cycle well ahead. If they do the same thing, you have both simply given margin away.

Reading the payoffs

  • Both hold price (+4, +4). Buyers renew at close to list on both sides. Nobody wins share, and both of you book a solid year.
  • One undercuts, the other holds. The one who cuts takes volume and finishes the cycle at +7. The one who held loses share and ends up discounting late to keep what is left, finishing at −2.
  • Both undercut (+1, +1). Everyone keeps roughly the customers they started with, at a materially worse price. Note that this is not a tie at zero. A price war does not destroy you, it just quietly costs you most of what the year could have been.

Rounds 3 and 5: the big renewals

Two of your buyers are far larger than the rest, and their contracts come up in cycles three and five. In those two rounds every payoff is doubled.

Immediately before each of those rounds, you may send one representative to meet one representative from the other side for two minutes. The meeting happens only if both sides ask for it. If either side declines, there is no meeting. Nothing said in that room is binding in any way. It is talk, not a contract.

Rules

  • Six rounds, one per annual renewal cycle.
  • Three minutes with your own team before each round to agree your position. Decide one round at a time. Do not plan the whole six now.
  • Decisions are private. Give your choice to the facilitator when asked. Both sides are revealed together, and the scores go on the board at the front.
  • No contact with the other side at any point, except in the two-minute meetings before rounds three and five, and only if both sides have agreed to meet.
  • Your goal is to finish with the largest total. Not to beat the other side, and not to be liked. The largest total.
Your objective
Finish the six cycles with as much margin as you can.
