Slide 2 of 5: This module

Four pictogram tiles: the first rule of game theory; game number one, I'm Thinking of a Number; the Battle of Wits; game number two, Guess Two-Thirds of the Average.

Slide 3 of 5: It's not (just) about you!

A figure of two people, You and Them, each with a thought bubble containing the other. Caption: whatever you do affects both sides, whatever they do affects both sides, and you both know it. This is the common knowledge requirement.

Slide 4 of 5: Using common knowledge to find equilibrium

A photograph of traffic signals over a busy road. Caption, revealed in two beats: I know to stop on red and go on green, and you know that I know it. So we get a nice equilibrium of stop-and-go traffic.

Slide 5 of 5: Except sometimes game theory sucks

The same photograph cropped close on the signals, and every one of them shows red and green lit at the same time. Caption: you can actually file lawsuits over this equilibrium failure.
