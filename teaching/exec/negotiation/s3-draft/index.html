What a fixed pie teaches

BATNA
  • Your BATNA anchors your resistance point
The bargaining zone
  • The area between the buyer’s and the seller’s resistance points
  • It is where agreement can be found
Opening offer
  • The first offer anchors the negotiation
  • Exaggerated offers get higher settlements, but…
  • Don’t begin with your target or your resistance point
Concessions
  • Two or three rounds
  • These benefit intangible factors: perceptions of trust, fairness and so on
SESSION 3 · CLAIMING VALUE

The power of anchoring

When considering a decision, the mind gives disproportionate weight to the first information it receives
Countering an anchor
  • Think through your position before you negotiate
  • Seek information from a variety of sources about the true value of what you are negotiating for
  • Think about the true value of the item to you
Where else have you seen anchoring at work?

SESSION 3 · CLAIMING VALUE

Distributive negotiation tactics

  • Determine your own (and assess the other’s) target, resistance point and costs of terminating
  • Prepare! Ask questions!
  • Change the other’s perception of the bargaining zone and their position in it
  • Manage the other’s impressions: control how much information you give
  • Don’t give clues to your motivation for selling or buying, and don’t say things that undermine value
  • Modify the other’s impressions: make your demands seem more attractive, and theirs less
  • Seller: “This space could be really useful to you because of…” Buyer: “This is probably the best offer you will get”
  • Manipulate the cost of delay, mention other buyers, set exploding offers or make threats

Where bluffing ends and lying begins

Deception: did anyone use it?
  • It can involve bluffing: “My agent advised me not to sell for more than…”
  • Or outright lying: “I have another buyer offering me…”
  • What are the risks? And don’t become emotionally involved
Misleading a regulator
  • In 2014, Facebook told the European Commission it could not reliably match Facebook and WhatsApp accounts
  • In 2017 the Commission fined it €110 million: the means existed in 2014, and Facebook’s staff knew it

When Apple stopped needing Microsoft to lose

  • In 1997, after years of fighting over patents, Apple and Microsoft settled several issues in one agreement
  • Microsoft would keep making Office for the Mac and invest $150 million in Apple, and the two companies would license each other’s patents
  • Steve Jobs: “we have to let go of the notion that for Apple to win, Microsoft has to lose”

A complaint handled by position

  • In 2008, baggage handlers at Chicago were seen throwing Dave Carroll’s guitar. For nearly a year he tried to get United to pay for the repair
  • United passed him from person to person, then said it would not compensate him. So he wrote a song about it
  • In the end, at his request, United gave $3,000 to a jazz school. The money was only part of what he wanted

SESSION 3 · CREATING VALUE

The Cold War, in brief

  • Dennis Roeper ran a small fleet of ice cream trikes in Salem, with a rule: if another seller is already parked, turn your music down, wave, and move on
  • From 2009, Efrain Escobar, selling from a minivan, followed Dennis’s drivers, parked in front of them and took their customers
  • Dennis complained to the police, confronted Efrain in a driveway, then had his drivers sell at half price wherever Efrain stopped
  • One night Efrain’s van was set on fire. The police never solved it, but rumours blamed Dennis’s side
  • In 2012, Dennis’s drivers boxed Efrain in at a park, then chased him across town, stop after stop
  • In 2015, over coffee, Dennis proposed a joint hub in Salem, since both of them drove to Portland every week for stock. They shook hands for the first time

SESSION 3 · TAKEAWAYS

a few key takeaways...

Know the zone
Your BATNA anchors your resistance point, and theirs anchors the other end
First offers anchor
Ambitious openings get higher settlements, but never open with your target or your resistance point
Bluffing or lying?
Know where the line is, and the risks of crossing it
Beneath positions
Interests explain what people want, and why
Dual concerns
Long-term relationships need both the substance and the relationship
Why not just compromise? Look for what serves both sides first
