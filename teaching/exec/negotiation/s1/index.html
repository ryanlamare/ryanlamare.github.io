What is negotiation?

A form of decision making in which two or more parties attempt to achieve their (opposing) interests
Though we negotiate every day, most of us know little about the strategy and psychology of effective negotiation

SESSION 1 · PRINCIPLES

Characteristics of negotiations

1
There are two or more parties that need each other (interdependence)
2
Something needs to be resolved: a sale, a conflict, a problem, an issue or a decision
3
There is no fixed solution
4
They involve give and take, and mutual adjustment
5
They involve the management of “intangibles”
6
They involve “dilemmas of honesty” and “dilemmas of trust”

Distributive
GoalsA negative correlation between both parties’ goal attainments
AssumptionConflicting interests
PurposeTo “claim value” (divide the pie)
Integrative
GoalsA positive correlation between both parties’ goal attainments

SESSION 1 · DEBRIEF

The Strategic Negotiations framework

1
Context
Hopes, fears, power and history
2
Structure
Is this a one-time negotiation or part of an ongoing relationship? Are people representing themselves, or are agents involved?
3
Process
Did your process realise the integrative potential of the issues? Was it fair to both parties? Were biases present?
4
Strategy
Distributive or integrative? Is it better to not reach a settlement at all (BATNA)?
5
Outcomes
Have both sides’ interests been addressed? Can the agreement be implemented? Have relationships been preserved?
Let’s apply your experience of Sally Soprano to the Strategic Negotiations framework

SESSION 1 · TAKEAWAYS

a few key takeaways...

What goes wrong?
Most often, we don’t even try to negotiate, or we prepare the wrong way
Know your BATNA
Every negotiation has an alternative, so know yours and think about theirs
Two main types
Distributive negotiation claims value, and integrative negotiation creates it
Why such different deals?
The same singer, the same role, and agreements far apart
A framework
Context, structure, process, strategy and outcomes
Effective negotiation is not only a list of things to do, it’s also a way of thinking
