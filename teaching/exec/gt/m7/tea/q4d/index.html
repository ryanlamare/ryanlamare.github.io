Who really meant it?

Hartwell’s
“Agree to the rise, or our lorries stop on Monday.”
What carrying it out would have costA quarter of its sales, a new packing line standing idle, and shoppers switching brands for good
Selwood’s
“Drop the rise, or your tea comes off our shelves.”
What carrying it out would have costOne in ten Hartwell’s buyers doing their whole weekly shop elsewhere, with no own-label tea for six months
Neither side would have carried out its threat
Strategic moves are only as valuable as they are credible. Carrying out either threat would have cost more than giving ground, so neither was credible, however firmly it was made.
