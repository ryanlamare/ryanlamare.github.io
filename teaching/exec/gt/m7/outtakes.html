Slide 4 of 5: Let's have an auction

The rules slide as it stood before the plain pass of 20 Sep 2026, slide 21 of 29. I am auctioning ten points. On your phones, whenever you like: bid, at least one point above the standing bid, and every bid goes up on the screen with your name on it. When the bidding stops, it is sold: the highest bidder pays their bid and takes the ten. The second-highest bidder also pays their bid, and gets nothing. A QR code to the auction, beside a box: the pot, ten points. The live board that followed it, Going once, is at the git tag m7-before-plain.

The strategic move: change that game in your favor by making a strategic move, commitment, threat or promise. Credibility: what would give the move credibility? The downside: are there potential downsides to making the move? A QR code to the phone page at ryanlamare.com/go and a count of ships, which here stays at zero.
