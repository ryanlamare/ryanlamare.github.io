IN YOUR WORLD · PART ONE

Finding a game in your world

Pick one real situation at work where you can’t get what you want without considering your best response to others, then answer four questions:
1
What prevents you from acting unilaterally to get what you want?
2
Who is the other player or group of players in the game?
3
What do you need to know about the others to achieve your goal?
4
What do you think they know about you that matters in this game?
AT YOUR TABLES: Brainstorm together, pick the situation with the most at stake, and choose one person to keep notes and share.
