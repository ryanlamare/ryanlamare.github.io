Build your branch

In pairs: one strategic interaction from your world, something you need from someone who can say no. One of you brings it; the other plays the other side.
THE YES YOU NEED
I need ___ from ___Real, small, and something you could actually ask for next week or next month: a signature, a sign-off, a price, a person, a date. Then tell your partner who they are playing, in thirty seconds
YOU OPEN
How do you open? Two or three ways, tap the one you takeIt lands on your partner’s phone; the rest are pruned
THEY REPLY
Your partner answers as that person would: yes, counter, or noA counter keeps the game going. A no ends the branch, so open too big and you might just get a straight no. Nobody gets a yes on the first ask
YOUR NEXT MOVE
Given their counter, what do you do? Then their reply again, and now they can say yesA yes reaches it. Two rounds, a third if you need one
