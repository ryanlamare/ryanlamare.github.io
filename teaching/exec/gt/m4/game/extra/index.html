PRICE WARS AT The junction go national

Fuel-price app arrives in town, putting both stations' signs on every phone within twenty miles · fortunes could be made, or lost, in the next two weeks!

The quiet duel beteen Aura and Buco's at the crossroads is a secret no longer. FuelWatch, the price app sweeping the region, has just added the junction to its map. From this Monday morning, every driver within twenty miles now sees both signs, live, before choosing where to fill up.

What it means is simple, and it is dynamite for the two stores. Undercut your rival on price and the app sends every car on every road to your forecourt: the week’s margin is quadrupled, seventy-two thousand pounds. The undercut station keeps only its two thousand of regulars, the handful who never look at a sign or use an app.

But, if the stores match each other the app has nothing interesting to show to customers. Two signs at £1.50 still pay £12,000 each. Two at £1.40 still pay £9,000 each. And, in an exclusive report, this newspaper can confirm that the week 6 doubling has been called off: these rules replace it, for both of the final two weeks.

Our reporter has discoverd that due to the app coming to town, an emergency meeting is in the cards between the two stations. Before week 5, the stations may meet one last time, but only if both agree to meet. Our reporter notes that there are no further meetings beyond Week 5 for either station.

Notice to both stations
  • Undercut alone: your margin is quadrupled, +£72k for the week. The other side gets £2k.
  • Match each other: £12k each at £1.50, £9k each at £1.40. No doubling anymore.
  • One last meeting before week 5, two minutes, only if both stations agree. Then silence.
Make it count.
