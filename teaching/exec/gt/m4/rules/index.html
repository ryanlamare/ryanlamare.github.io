Price Wars: two stations, one junction

Aura Fuels
Buco’s Petrol

Identical fuel, identical drivers. Each week you post £1.50 or £1.40. Drivers go to the cheaper sign; match, and you share them.

One ordinary week at the junction, in thousands of pounds. Aura’s revenue first, Buco’s second.

How we play

  • Six weeks. Every Monday you post your price without seeing theirs. Then both signs go up, and everybody sees everything.
  • One phone for your team. Agree your price, lock it in, and hold the phone up when your junction is called. Once it is locked it cannot be changed.
  • Then tap what the other station posted. Your phone keeps your revenue.
  • Weeks 3 and 6 pay double. Before each one you may ask for a two-minute meeting, one person from each station. It happens only if both stations ask, and nothing said in it binds anyone.
  • Otherwise, no talking to the other station.
Your sole objective: maximize your own station’s revenue over the six weeks.
