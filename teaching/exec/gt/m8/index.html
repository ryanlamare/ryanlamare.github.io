Manipulating information

Reveal selected information truthfully

MODULE 8

Signaling, signal jamming, and screening

Three devices can be used to manipulate information:
Seinfeld, the rental-car deskthree nested asymmetries in one scene
Signaling
Favorable leakage of information, or actions taken to have the other side believe your information is good
Signal jamming
Acting to reduce or eliminate the leakage of unfavorable information
Screening
Forcing the other side to reveal that side’s true information
MODULE 8 · LET’S PLAY

A car market game

  • Half of you have been allocated as car buyers and half are car sellers
  • There are two types of cars available in this market: lemons (bad quality cars) and peaches (good quality cars)
  • Buyers care about the quality of the car and will pay a premium for good quality
  • Assume that no further information is available to any side other than what’s written on your phones
  • Also keep in mind that sellers want to get as much as possible irrespective of whether the car is a lemon or a peach
  • Sellers should show buyers what’s written on their phones when buyers approach them
  • Try to make a deal!

Adverse selection

People know their own propensity for risk far better than insurance companies do
The pool, priced at the average
→
Only the bad risks stay
People select themselves into groups based on their private information, and by doing so reveal it. So companies offer several choices as a screening mechanism: a risky driver, offered low premiums for low coverage or high premiums for high coverage, selects into the second

MODULE 8 · ROLE

Committee Member

  • You’re an honest member of the committee, here in good faith to recommend the best offer for the airline
  • Your main job: spot the committee members with a hidden agenda and get them removed before they take the committee over
  • You have no special power, just the discussion and your read of people. Who is steering the committee toward a bidder they have already chosen?
  • If your phone doesn’t give you a special role, you’re a Committee Member
Who are the Manipulators?
MODULE 8 · ROLE

Manipulator

  • By day you look like any other member, but you secretly back one bidder, whatever the offers say
  • Your phone shows you which bidder, and who your fellow Manipulators are
  • Each night, together, you make an anonymous allegation against one honest member, and the airline pulls them off the committee while it’s investigated. If you can’t agree on a name, nobody is removed that night
  • You win if Manipulators are half the committee, but you lose if you get caught, so never reveal your agenda!
But I’m a committee member!
MODULE 8 · ROLE

Auditor

  • You are an honest committee member who can check one committee member each night for a hidden agenda
  • Name one person on your phone, and it tells you whether they are a Manipulator
  • Your read is always accurate, but you can’t prove it, and a Manipulator can claim to be you
  • Say who you are, and you risk being the next name in an anonymous allegation
One check a night, and it never lies
MODULE 8 · ROLE

General Counsel

  • The one member who can shield a committee member, so that any allegation against them that night is dismissed
  • Each night, name one person on your phone. Your shield dismisses any allegation made against them
  • You can’t shield the same person two nights in a row, but you may shield yourself
  • Keeping the Auditor in the room is often your most important job, if you can spot who they are!
One shield a night

MODULE 8 · GUESS TWO-THIRDS

Finding the Nash equilibrium in the two-thirds game

  • How do we solve this game? Remember common knowledge and successive elimination of inferior strategies
  • Let’s say everyone in the room guessed 100…what should you guess?
  • 100 × (2/3) = 67, so we can eliminate numbers 68 or above
  • But if nobody will play above 67, your winning strategy should be 2/3 of those choices
  • 67 × (2/3) = 45, so we can now eliminate numbers 46 or above
  • And so on (30, 20, 13, 9…)…all the way down to 0, which is the Nash equilibrium
  • So, all of those who answered 0 got it right…or did they??
