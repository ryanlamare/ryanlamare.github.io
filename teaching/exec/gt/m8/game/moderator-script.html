APPLIED GAME THEORY · MODULE 8 · CLOSED SESSION
Moderator script
The Tapas committee

Tapas Airways is choosing who will finance its spare engine. The people at your table are the committee that recommends a bidder to the board. Hidden among them are the Backers, who secretly back one of the four bidders whatever the offers say. Your page tells you which. Your job is to run the table; the clock on the big screen runs the room, so start and end each phase when Ryan calls it.

Before the first night

  • Count the table. Ten or eleven: two Backers. Twelve to fifteen: three. Always one Auditor and one General Counsel; everyone else is a Committee Member.
  • Phones deal the roles when you tap DEAL ROLES on your moderator page (ryanlamare.com/teaching/exec/gt/m8/game/mod.html, then pick your table). Your page then lists every seat with its role, and only you can see it. If the phones are not behaving, use the printed role cards instead and keep this sheet as your record.
  • Everyone introduces themselves. Then Ryan calls the first night.

Night, on phones

Say: Night falls. Phones only, no talking, and no looking at your neighbour’s screen.

  • Your page shows three lines: Backers agreed (or still deciding), Auditor has checked, General Counsel has cleared. Everyone else is writing their notes.
  • When the three lines are ticked, or the clock runs out, tap END NIGHT. The page tells you who was named in the complaint and whether General Counsel’s clearance dismissed it.
  • If the Backers never agreed, nobody is removed. Say so in the morning; it is the rule, not an error.

Night, by pointing (the fallback if phones fail)

  1. Everyone close your eyes. Keep some noise going: tap the table, hum, so nobody can hear movement.
  2. Backers, open your eyes and find each other. Wait. Point together at the one person you want off the committee. You must agree. Note the name. Backers, close your eyes.
  3. General Counsel, open your eyes. Point at the person you clear tonight. Not the same person as last night. Note the name. General Counsel, close your eyes.
  4. Auditor, open your eyes. Point at the person you want checked. Thumbs up if a Backer, thumbs down if honest. Auditor, close your eyes.
  5. Everyone open your eyes. Then the morning announcement below.

Morning

If the complaint stands: Overnight an anonymous complaint was filed against [name]. Under the airline’s conflict rules I have to remove them from the committee. They leave the table but keep their phone.

If General Counsel cleared that person: A complaint was filed overnight, but General Counsel had cleared the person named, so it is dismissed. Nobody leaves. Do not say who was named.

Day

  • Five minutes on the big-screen clock. The committee debates which offer is best and, above all, who is not acting in good faith.
  • An accusation needs a second. The accused gets thirty seconds. Then tap OPEN VOTE: everyone votes recuse or keep on their phone and the votes land on the big screen by name. Majority recuses; a tie keeps them.
  • One vote a day. Anyone recused leaves the table and keeps their phone.

The end

The committee wins

Every Backer has been recused. The committee makes a clean recommendation on the merits.

The Backers win

Backers are half the seats still at the table. They can steer the airline to their bidder.

Tap GAME OVER on your page, then send everyone to the big screen for the suspicion vote and the reveal. Ten points to everyone on the winning side; the scoreboard works that out from the log.
