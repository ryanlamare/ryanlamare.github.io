MODULE 6 · THE PENALTY SHOOTOUT

Let’s play: the penalty shootout

  • Pair up with the person next to you, on your phones
  • Ten kicks each, taken in turns: you shoot, then you go in goal
  • On every kick you both choose left or right at the same time, without seeing the other’s choice
  • If the keeper dives the way you shoot, it’s saved. If not, it’s a goal

MODULE 6 · THE INSPECTION GAME

Let’s play: the inspection game

  • Pair up. One of you is the safety regulator, the other runs an engine maintenance shop. You swap halfway
  • Eight rounds. Each round, at the same time: the regulator inspects the shop or trusts the paperwork; the shop does the full check or skips a step
  • Inspections cost the regulator money. If the engine shop skips safety steps, it saves them time and money. But getting caught skipping a step results in a fine and grounds the engine

MODULE 6 · THE INSPECTION GAME

So how should a regulator behave?

  • Constantly inspecting every tiny step is impractical at scale
  • Always trusting the paperwork incentivises the shop to skip steps, so the shop needs to be at least occasionally inspected to keep it honest
  • The game has no pure strategy equilibrium
  • The ideal strategy is to inspect at least 20% of the time, which keeps compliance at 90% or more
  • The regulator’s job is not to be everywhere. It is to be unpredictable
