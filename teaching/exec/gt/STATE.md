## Make me an offer, decided by Ryan in the room (his idea, 21 Sep 2026; BUILT the same day, awaiting his run)

Rehearsing module 1 with a room of bots, Ryan found the offers game too complicated and too much on the phones: people pressed a button and a script answered. A short press accepts and a long press (half a second, red runs across while it is held) rejects, on a name or on a column's number; ACCEPT THE REST and REJECT THE REST settle whoever is left. His order in the room: one person first (talk to Priya about her £50, then accept), then a column, then the rest; the same in game two, on the same board, empty again. How it works, for whoever touches it next: the wire is unchanged (`name|o|amt`, `name|d|amt|a-or-r|phase`, `=game|p|N`) plus one new marker, `=game|r|N`, posted at the reveal; the `p` marker is posted when the QR step shows, the `r` marker when the board slide becomes the active one and there is an offer to show.
