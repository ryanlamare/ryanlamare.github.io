# Azee Tournament — run sheet

LER 565, Week 6 live session. Paper fallback for `bracket.html`. Everything below
works with nothing but the Google Sheet and this page.

**Format:** three rounds of simultaneous 1-v-1 games, then a top-4 knockout.
Every student plays all three rounds. Nobody is eliminated before the semis.

---

## Run of show

| | Block | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | FLEX course evaluations — flex.illinois.edu | ~5 min |
| 2 | Week 6 content recap — strategic moves | ~10 min |
| 3 | Sign-in: everyone logs into azee.mattle.online, puts **name + username** in the Sheet | ~5 min |
| 4 | Rounds 1–3 | ~21 min (3 × 7) |
| 5 | Semi-finals (two games) | ~7 min |
| 6 | Final — screen-shared, everyone watches | ~7 min |
| 7 | Wrap: what's due Sunday Aug 9 | ~5 min |

Each round is one 5-minute fast game plus ~2 minutes to open the room, create the
game, report the result, and come back. **If you're running late, cut round 3** —
two rounds still seeds a top 4 (see tiebreaks below).

---

## Rules — read these out, or paste into chat

1. All games are at **azee.mattle.online**. Log in and grab your username.
2. Put your **name and username in the Google Sheet**. That's how results are tracked.
3. You'll be paired and sent to a breakout room each round. **The lower-numbered
   player creates the game**: normal board, **fast / 5 min**.
4. Play. When it's done, **the winner types the result in the Sheet** and both of
   you come back to the main room.
5. **You play all three rounds regardless of whether you win.** No one is knocked out.
6. After three rounds, the **four best records** play the semi-finals; the winners
   play the final while everyone watches.
7. You never play the same opponent twice.

## Picking the top 4

Rank by **wins**. Then, in order:

1. **Head-to-head** — if two tied players met, the winner goes above.
2. **Fewer byes** — a bye counts as a win, but a real win beats a walkover.
3. **Your call.** With 14 players over 3 rounds a multi-way tie at 2–1 is likely.
   Pick whoever you like — it's a game, not a ranking exercise. Coin-flip on
   camera is a perfectly good answer and the room will enjoy it.

**Seed the semis 1 v 4 and 2 v 3.** Winners meet in the final.

If you cut round 3 for time, everything above still works — you'll just have more
ties to break, and probably a 3-way or 4-way tie at 2–0.

## When things go sideways

- **Someone drops mid-tournament.** Their opponent takes the win for that round.
  Re-number nobody; just leave the gap and give byes where the missing number
  comes up.
- **A game overruns.** 5-minute fast games shouldn't, but call it: whoever is
  ahead on the board wins, or flip for it. Don't let one room hold up six others.
- **A pair can't get a game started.** Send them to a fresh breakout room, or have
  them both play the next round instead and carry a 0–0 for the missed one.
- **Too few for a top 4** (fewer than about 8 in the room) — go straight from the
  rounds to a **final between the top 2**. Skip the semis.
- **azee.mattle.online is down.** Fall back to any Week 1–5 game in
  `teaching/ler565/*/games/` — they run offline in the browser and need no
  accounts. The tournament structure above is game-agnostic.
