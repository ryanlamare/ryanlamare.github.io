What changes for Rounds 5 and 6

Team. Project Titan has drawn extraordinary press attention, and the whole industry is now watching. The Board has decided to tie our final two sprints to the company’s two flagship events of the year: the Spring Keynote (Round 5) and the Fall Keynote (Round 6). At each one, the world sees both teams’ devices side by side, on the same stage.

If one device clearly pulls ahead at a launch — that is, your team pushes to Grind Mode while the other holds at Normal Hours — it wins the headline demo, the press, and the industry’s attention. The reward for that standout is quadrupled. If both devices push hard, or both play it safe, no single product steals the show and the rewards stay exactly as before.

The new rule, Rounds 5 & 6
Pull ahead alone at a launch and your reward is QUADRUPLED: +$24M. A shared spotlight is worth nothing extra.

What changes for Rounds 5 & 6

  • Pull ahead alone (you Grind, they hold Normal): your reward jumps to +$24M; the other team stays at −$2M.
  • Both push hard (Grind / Grind): no standout, so $0 / $0, as before.
  • Both play it safe (Normal / Normal): +$3M / +$3M, as before.
  • These rules apply only to the final two rounds. There is no contact between teams once Round 6 begins, and Round 6 is the end.

One last conversation

Before Round 5, each team may send one representative to meet the other side, a single time, and only if both teams agree to it. After that, there is no further contact. Whatever is said is not binding. Choose your move, and your moment, carefully.

Make it count.
